HoldLight: Making Indoor Climbing More Accessible for Blind and Low-Vision Climbers through Layered Route Preview and Real-Time Next-Hold Guidance

Chenyu Du, Junyan He, Jiafei Sun, Yue Li

PDFVideoProcess portfolio

HoldLight combines layered pre-climb route previews and real-time next-hold audio guidance to support blind and low-vision climbers, with explicit fallback when the system is uncertain.

More about this work

I led the end-to-end mobile system implementation, including wall capture, hold detection, route sequencing, pose tracking, and audio guidance. I also led formative inquiry, iterative pilot refinement, and a counterbalanced evaluation with 12 blind and low-vision climbers.

The study examined route understanding, uncertainty, and perceived autonomy compared with controlled on-demand human support. Human safety support remains available throughout the climbing workflow.

RoboBlockly Studio: Conversational Block Programming with Embodied Robot Feedback for Computational Thinking

Leyi Li*, Chenyu Du*, Jiafei Sun, Erick Purwanto, Qing Zhang

PDFDOIarXiv

A learning environment that brings together block-based programming, conversational AI, and a physical robot. Learners author, run, observe, and revise programs to connect abstract code with tangible outcomes.

More about this work

As co-first author, I co-led the technical design and implementation: Blockly-to-Python conversion, task-grounded AI feedback, WebSocket deployment, and execution on a Jetson Orin Nano / ROS 2 robot.

We informed the design through interviews with five programming teachers and evaluated the system with 32 high-school programming novices. I contributed to both study design and execution.
