ACM ASSETS 2026 · First author

HoldLight

Accessible indoor climbing through layered previews and timely audio guidance.

Helping blind and low-vision climbers understand a route before climbing and request guidance during movement.

My contribution
Led system development, formative inquiry, and a counterbalanced mixed-method study with 12 blind and low-vision climbers.
Research finding
Higher route-understanding scores and fewer requests for routine human route information than with controlled on-demand human support.

ACM DIS 2026 · Co-first author

RoboBlockly Studio

Making program behavior tangible through conversational AI and a physical robot.

A learning environment connecting code authoring, execution, observation, and debugging.

My contribution
Co-led technical implementation and contributed to study design and execution: five instructor interviews and a study with 32 novice high-school students.
Research finding
The AI+Robot condition revealed a shift toward execution-based error checking and more targeted AI queries.
