Selected research

ACM ASSETS 2026 · First author

HoldLight

Layered route previews and next-hold audio guidance for blind and low-vision climbers.

My contribution
Conceived and led system development; designed and conducted a counterbalanced mixed-method study with 12 blind and low-vision climbers.
Research finding
In a controlled indoor study, participants had higher route-understanding scores and needed fewer routine human information-support interventions than with on-demand human support. Human safety support remained available.
Read case study ↗ Paper Video Code

ACM DIS 2026 · Co-first author

RoboBlockly Studio

Block-based programming with conversational AI feedback and physical robot execution.

My contribution
Implemented the programming-to-robot pipeline and AI feedback; designed and conducted a counterbalanced study with 32 novice high-school students.
Research finding
Compared with AI-only interaction, observations in the AI+Robot condition showed more execution-based error checking and more targeted questions to the AI.
